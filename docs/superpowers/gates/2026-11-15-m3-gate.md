# M3 gate — assets (Plan 3)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-portfolio-assets-design.md` · Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-portfolio-plan-3-assets.md` · Branch: `plan-3/assets`. At most three rounds, like the earlier gates.

## Round 1 — agenda

**Built:**
- Live loops of eight projects: seven recorded from their sites (`npm run loops`) — ALTUS, AslanAir, Aslan-can, GenGO (English), Kolir (English), TUTI and Moto (Ukrainian, frames with little text) — and Gipper's editor, recorded from Rustam's own Chrome: his demo layout, the athletes grid → "Gregory 1" → a font change, cropped to the editor. 10–11 s each, seamless, WebM + MP4, 0.7–1.5 MB per file, 15 MB in all; each project's still is its loop's first frame.
- On the wall, the TV in front of the camera plays its loop; the others keep their stills. Leaving Work, opening a case or a drop to the low tier stops it. Phones keep stills on the wall.
- In a case card the loop plays over its still, muted, on every screen (phones included).
- No video under reduced motion, with the browser's save-data hint, with `?loops=0`, or without WebGL; the first load asks for no video.
- About opens with the portrait as a street poster: neon duotone (About's cyan), torn edges, two strips of tape, a slight tilt; it swings in with the boxes.

**Not recorded:**
- DevDigest: it needs Docker Postgres, `.env` files and API keys, so it keeps its current still.

**For Rustam to look at (laptop and phone; Ctrl+Shift+R first):**
- Each loop on its TV and in its case: what it shows, its pace, the seam.
- The loops' colour on the wall: the final review found them washed out (a double sRGB encode in the screen shader) and a black frame at the switch from still to video; both are fixed, and the switch should now be invisible.
- The poster: the duotone's tone, its size and place above About's title.

**Verification:**
- Lint and typecheck clean; 135 unit tests.
- e2e: chromium 130/131 in the last full run — the one red, a case test clicking before the scene was ready (no loop involved), now waits for the scene and passes 3/3, its spec 16/16; 20/20 on a Pixel 7.
- prod: 2/2, JS on `/en` 805 KB (was ~801).
- perf: a Work stop with its loop 27.8 ms against 27.7 without. The whole-journey probe swings between 27.9 and 34.6 ms on this machine whatever is switched off, so it needs re-measuring on an idle machine (Plan 4).

**Final review (opus):** three fixes, each test-first: the wall's video colour, a black frame at the reveal, and a refused autoplay in the case card (iOS Low Power Mode). Seven minors deferred to Plan 4 (in the plan's ledger and the rulings doc at merge).

**Found on the way:**
- Every loop first failed in Chrome after its first frame: the files were full-range, from the screencast's JPEG frames. They are now limited-range BT.709, and a test checks this.

## Round 1 — 2026-11-15

**Rustam (nine points, with references):**
1. The Gipper loop lost the editor's left panel — a key part of the interface.
2. While the start screen loads, hide the header's buttons and the text at the bottom: only the loader, so nothing gets pressed.
3. The loader is too plain; make it more interesting.
4. After the first scroll the pole with its signs and the traffic light should move to the right and stay as an interactive element, and scrolling should feel like moving forward along the road (reference: a first-person ride down a wet neon avenue).
5. Work: move the TVs from the street into a shop in the building on the left, in its window; scrolling to them, the camera drives up to the window and the TVs show the work as now.
6. About: as if written on the pole.
7. Stack: signs hanging over the road (overhead highway gantry signs, a cluster of route signs).
8. "Let's go" (Contact): a big billboard (cyberpunk street billboards).
9. A 404 page: a red warning triangle, "CRITICAL ERROR", glitching on black.

**Done in this round:**
- 1: the Gipper loop shows the whole editor, its left panel included; the strips above and below are the editor's own colours.
- 2: while the scene loads, the header, the hero, the Next button and the progress tube stay hidden until the street (or the fallback) is there; without scripts nothing waits.

**Next:** points 3–9 change the scene itself (a ride down the street, a shop window, text in the world, a billboard, the loader, a 404 page). They go through a brainstorm and a new spec rather than into Plan 3.
