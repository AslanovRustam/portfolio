## Round 3 — 2026-11-12

**Rustam:** everything else is OK; the wet asphalt is not "wow" yet. References: https://ukrnafta-race-game.vercel.app/ (the link missing since the brainstorm) and https://cyberpunk-race.vercel.app/.

**What the references do** (screenshots `.superpowers/look/ref-*.png`): the whole road is wet, every light throws a long vertical streak toward the viewer, many light sources sit low near the horizon (shop fronts, neon, lamps), and it rains. Our signs hang ~3 m up while the camera looks level, so their mirror images landed below the frame — the asphalt had nothing to reflect in view.

**Changes:**
- Street furniture behind the pole (`streetLayout`, unit-tested): lamp rows on both kerbs with cones of light in the rain, neon blade signs facing the viewer, long wall signs. Emissive geometry only, no real lights.
- Reflection streaks (`reflectionStreaks` + `streakPlacement`, unit-tested): one additive streak per light, laid each frame on the road line from the light's base toward the camera, centred where the mirror image lands (h/(H+h) of the way), soft at both ends and broken into ripple threads. A blurred planar mirror smeared these small, far lights into nothing, so the streaks carry the effect.
- Asphalt: wet everywhere (glossy film 0.05–0.45 roughness, darker tarmac), the reflector's depth fade off, anisotropic blur and a ripple distortion map.
- Rain: GPU line streaks replace the dust particles.

**Shots:** `.superpowers/look/g3f-0.26.png`, `.superpowers/look/g3f-0.48.png`. **Perf:** median 27.8 ms (36 fps) at the medium tier on the integrated GPU, unchanged. **Tests:** unit 50/50, e2e 31/31, mobile 2/2.
